### Any device, names, and reporting abuse
Links, Backup, Saved Surf, Snapshots and Cloud Surf are managed from any device you log in on (needs your dinons). Only up- links are end-to-end; the rest is client-side encrypted. New up- links are also kept in Links as ciphertext, so c-N copies them on any device. Names are stored encrypted (e1.…).
Every link page shows who shared it (mail + d3) with a Copy button and a Report abuse link to <your worker>/help. Reports about pirated, adult, illegal or harmful content go to the admin, who can restrict the link or suspend the account.

print  - capture the screen as drawn (copy to clipboard or save as txt); works in every menu.
loadtime [N|all|full] - what consumed the time since the previous screen, ranked, with network timings. VK_LOADTIME=0 disables.
cc     - cheat codes: a list of every command the current prompt accepts. =lo filters it, pick a row and the command is typed into the prompt for you; nothing runs until you press Enter (c- waits for its numbers: c-1-5). Works in every menu and prompt.
