CAUSE
Each menu re-typed its own footer text; the label of a generic key lived in dozens of places and no test compared footer keys with the maps, so copies drifted.

FIX
engine/bashbasicsbyvk_menuchrome.sh: new _MC_KEYLABEL table (u=Back q=Quit z=Cancel rf=Refresh); _mc_footer takes a bare key ("u") and prints its canonical label, "key:Label" still works for menu-specific keys. 19 call sites in auth, api, csv, adf_selection, adf_gdrive, upgrade, transfer, bundle_io, find and staging_buffer now pass bare keys. tests/cmd_drift.sh gained three checks: every footer key is wired in some map or listed as loop-local; no file retypes u:Back / q:Quit / z:Cancel / rf:Refresh; bare keys render their canonical label next to a custom one. Left as they are on purpose: u:Up and u:Up parent (parent-folder navigation, a different meaning from Back). Still hand-typed and not changed: u:back x10, rf:refresh x6, u:Exit x6, q:Close x4, u:cancel, u:up/back, rf:Reload; the case-only ones can move to bare keys without a visible change, the others change wording and need the owner's call.
