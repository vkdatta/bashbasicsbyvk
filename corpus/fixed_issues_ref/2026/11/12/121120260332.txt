1. ISSUE IN BRIEF
In the delete flow, u at "Back up before deleting" did not go back to "Confirm delete": it left the whole delete and landed on the main file list. The same flow ended on the file list from u at Confirm delete (instead of the item picker) and, in delete by date / extension / CSV and the stand-alone delete command, u at Confirm or Backup left the flow instead of going one question back. Follow-up of BB-101120260205-1 / -2 / -3 (Find, rename, create, transfer were fixed; the delete family was missed).

1. HOW DID YOU DEBUG
1. Read corpus index (Menus & Navigation) and note 101120260205: the lesson there is "a flow with more than one question calls _mc_steps; never `|| return` after the first u".
2. grep for every caller of _lk_confirm_delete / _bk_before_delete: delete_items, _fxdel_run_selected (used by delete by date / ext / CSV / selected), the stand-alone `delete` command and two rules prompts. Each ran confirm, then backup, as two plain calls with `|| return`.
3. Read _bvk_menu (choose.sh): u and q both end in _bc_leave and both return 1 with an empty VAR, so a caller could not tell "back" from "cancel" at all.
4. Probed the real app in the pty harness (tests/paste_input/ptyapp.py) with backup_mode=ask: d, 1, y, u went to the file list on the untouched archive; after the fix u shows Confirm delete, a second u shows the item picker.
5. Added 4 cases to tests/nav_back/nav_test.py (3 u cases, 1 cancel case) and ran them on the untouched archive: the 3 u cases FAIL there and PASS on the fix, the cancel case passes on both (it must: c keeps the file).
Not verified by running: delete by date / extension / CSV and the stand-alone `delete` command (changed by reading, syntax-checked only); the GitHub, api, snapshot, auth-pay, upload-cost flows listed below were audited by reading and NOT changed.

1. CAUSE
Two causes. (a) _bvk_menu / _bvk_yn gave no way to tell u from q, so no chained flow could implement "one question back". (b) the delete gate was two sequential calls with `|| return`, not a step list, and _lk_confirm_delete itself chained up to four questions the same way.

- settings/bashbasicsbyvk_choose.sh: _bvk_menu records the key that left the box in _BVK_LEFT (u or q); new _bvk_back = "the last menu was left with u and no abort is running". Works with and without the command layer.
- functions/bashbasicsbyvk_lock.sh: _lk_ask (answered / back / cancelled); _lk_confirm_delete is now a step list (_lcd_first, _lcd_key_one, _lcd_choice, _lcd_final, _lcd_key_all) on _mc_steps; new _lk_delete_gate = Confirm delete then Back up, u = one step back, the selection is restored when u re-asks Confirm. Contract of _lk_confirm_delete: 0 go, 1 u, 2 q, 3 cancelled.
- functions/bashbasicsbyvk_action_confirm_key.sh: returns 2 for u (the callers treated every non-zero as "failed", unchanged).
- staging/!buffer/fileapi/3 backup/bashbasicsbyvk_staging_backup.sh: _bk_before_delete = steps (_bkd_ask, _bkd_name, _bkd_upload); returns 6 for u on the first question (0 go, 1 do not delete as before); u at "Delete anyway?" returns to the name prompt.
- functions/bashbasicsbyvk_delete.sh: delete_items loops: u at Confirm delete = the item picker again. loops/iloop/fx/file_fx: _fxdel_run_selected returns 1 for u; delete by date and by extension are step lists with Confirm as the last step; delete by CSV re-opens the CSV picker.
- cmd/bashbasicsbyvk_delete (stand-alone): three steps (_bd_confirm, _bd_lock, _bd_backup); engine/bashbasicsbyvk_yn gets -b (u exits 4 instead of 2); functions/_3bvk_import_cmd_delete.sh now also sources bashbasicsbyvk_menuchrome.sh (for _mc_steps).
- loadmap stubs regenerated.
Lesson: whenever a flow asks a second yes/no or menu, the first caller is a step list; read _bvk_back, never `|| return` after the first u.
