HOW DID YOU DEBUG
Read auth.sh (recharge, user menu), browse.sh / tpl_defs.sh (status row), notify.sh (the cached header count pattern) and the loadmap rules. Ran the plan helpers and the gradient in plain bash (values for 0..400 and bar fill counts), then python3 loadmap_gen --write / --check and bash tests/run.sh once (12/12 passed after removing the now-dead _bvk_credits_for).
